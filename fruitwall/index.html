FruitWall

Tape a banana to a gallery wall. Pick the wall, the tape and the light, let the critics name a price, and keep your masterpiece as a phone wallpaper. A free game for iPhone.

Support

Something not working, or an idea for the next object? Write to alec.maks@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Frequently asked

Why do I see an ad before saving a wallpaper?
The game is free. A short ad before each save pays for it. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the wallpaper is saved anyway.

Where are my wallpapers?
In the Photos app, in Recents. Open one and choose Use as Wallpaper.

The wallpaper did not save.
Allow FruitWall to add photos in Settings > FruitWall > Photos.

Privacy

FruitWall has no accounts and keeps your works on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
