Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

FruitWall is a free game for iPhone. You tape a fruit, a vegetable or an everyday object to a gallery wall, critics name a price, and you can save the result as a phone wallpaper. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

  • FruitWall has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.
  • Your artworks and any photo you use as a wall stay on your iPhone.
  • The game shows a short rewarded ad from Google AdMob before you save a wallpaper. Google collects some data to deliver that ad, as described below.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your gallery. Thumbnails, titles and prices of your last works are kept in the app's local storage on your iPhone. Delete them with the trash button in the gallery, or by deleting the app.
  • Photos you choose as a wall. If you pick a photo or take one with the camera, it is processed on your iPhone to build the wall texture. It is never uploaded.
  • Wallpapers you save. FruitWall asks for permission to add images to your Photos library. It only adds the wallpaper you save and cannot read your other photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Before a wallpaper is saved, the game shows a rewarded video ad served by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device information such as model, operating system and language,
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking,
  • ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Before the first ad, iOS asks whether FruitWall may track you across apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are also asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can review or withdraw that consent from the credits screen in the game (Ad privacy choices).

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

FruitWall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can use the choices above or contact Google through its privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
